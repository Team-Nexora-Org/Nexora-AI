1. PROBLEM CONTEXT

Real infrastructure projects already have structured project schedules in systems such as Primavera P6, Microsoft Project, or other PMIS platforms.

The project schedule contains planned L5/L6 activities.

However, actual execution information is distributed across multiple supervisors and disciplines.

Examples:

* Piping supervisor
* Civil supervisor
* Mechanical supervisor
* Electrical supervisor
* Instrumentation supervisor

They may report through:

* Voice
* Text
* Daily progress reports
* Excel
* Site diaries
* PDFs
* Photos

The terminology used by the field does not necessarily match the terminology used by the schedule.

Example:

Schedule:

“A10931 — Erect Spool Line 24-XX”

Field:

“Line 24-XX ka spool erection complete hai.”

Another:

“24-XX spool fixed and erection finished.”

Another:

“Piping team completed erection of the 24-XX spool.”

These statements can represent the same planned activity.

The problem is therefore not merely data entry.

The core problem is:

HETEROGENEOUS FIELD EXECUTION DATA MUST BE INTERPRETED AND RECONCILED AGAINST STRUCTURED L5/L6 PROJECT SCHEDULE ACTIVITIES.

The planner should not have to manually read every report, determine what happened, find the corresponding schedule activity, calculate progress, and update the schedule from scratch.

NEXORA is the intelligent reconciliation layer between field execution and the existing project schedule.
